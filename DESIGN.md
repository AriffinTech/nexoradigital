# Design Direction: Nexora Digital

**Identity**: Nexora Digital is a B2B partner for Smart Manufacturing Digitalization. 
**Personality**: Authoritative, industrial, precise, and transparent. We are an established engineering partner, not a trendy consumer SaaS. The design must communicate reliability, accuracy, and operational efficiency.
**Palette**: Green, White, and Black.
- **Background**: Crisp white and very light neutral grays for maximum readability and clean data presentation. (Avoid forced dark-mode unless requested).
- **Text & High-Contrast**: Deep black/slate for strong, legible typography.
- **Accent**: A solid, established engineering green (e.g., Emerald). Avoid neon or overly bright shades. Use green strictly to draw attention to primary actions (CTAs) and success states.

**Typography**: 
- Headings: Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter or a stable grotesque) conveying structure and weight.
- Body: Highly legible sans-serif optimized for reading technical copy and data.

**Mood**: Professional, grounded, high-contrast, and highly functional. The UI should feel like a precision instrument.

**Dials**:
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Balanced). The design should be confident and structured, not overly loud or experimental.
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Consistent with a few breaks). Layouts should reflect the structured nature of manufacturing, using a predictable grid with deliberate variation for key features.
- **MOTION**: 1 (Hover states only). This is a functional B2B tool. Motion should be limited to necessary micro-interactions (like button hovers) to keep the interface fast and distraction-free. No endless loops, floating elements, or scroll-jacking.
